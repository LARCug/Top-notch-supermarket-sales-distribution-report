# Top-notch-supermarket-sales-distribution-report
This project is focused on analysing the sales data  for Top -notch supermarket to gain insights that will drive sales and revenue 

![DASHBOARD 4](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a45b0578-6df0-4b45-8bf2-913689af8fc6)

INTRODUCTION

Every supermarket outfit have several goals upon establishing. Of them all, meeting customers needs,drive sales and revenue,establish quality customer loyalty and stay competitive among all competitors remains their sole goals. Generally speaking,every supermarket outfit aims to device actionable strategies to drive sales and generate profit. This project focuses solely on conducting an analysis report on the TOPNOTCH supermarket sales dataset to aid the owner tackle there current crises.

WHAT IS THE PROBLEM?

Overtime, Top-notch supermarket have been on top of experiencing low sales and needed to find what the issue could be. Using their sales data, actionable insights will be generated.

PROJECT GOAL

To devise strategies to drive sales and revenue growth

RECOMMENDATIONS

1)To build excellent customer service relationship,I recommend loyalty programs are implemented especially for women who patronise the store more. This inturn will increase customer retention and loyalty and attract more men to come shopping thereby increasing gratitude influx of customers from bothe genders

2)The sales operations manager should ensure there should be reliable availability of ATM machines of various banking systems to avoid unhealthy queue and loss of customers to their customers.

3)The store inventory keeper should ensure always that there is availability of food and beverage in stock to replenish when exhausted. This will boost sales and revenue growth

4)Health and beauty items are often expensive. There should be a price reduction on this item to foster sales, increase profitability in that sector thereby making the store competitive among it's colleagues.

5)Since there is an influx of customers by 7pm,offer promotions for morning, afternoon and evening purchases. Also target leverage on discount sales on products at specific times of the day. This will attract customers for all times of the days thereby increasing sales.
